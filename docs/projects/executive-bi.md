# Case Study 2: Executive BI Dashboard & KPI Architecture

## Executive Summary

Leadership across Sales, Product, and Finance struggled with fragmented, manual weekly reporting that took over 12 hours to assemble. This project established a unified business intelligence dashboard suite providing real-time visibility into executive KPIs, revenue trends, and operational funnel efficiency.

---

## Business Problem & Context

* **Stakeholders:** Chief Executive Officer, VP of Sales, VP of Product, Finance Leads
* **Core Challenge:** Multiple disparate data sources (Stripe, Google Analytics, CRM) resulted in conflicting metrics and slow executive decision-making.
* **Goal:** Build an automated, single source of truth BI dashboard with drill-down capabilities.

---

## Key Metrics & Impact

| Metric | Manual Process | Automated BI Suite | Benefit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weekly Reporting Time** | 12.5 Hours | 0.5 Hours | **12 Hours Saved / Week** |
| **Data Freshness** | 7-Day Delay | Real-Time / Hourly | **Instant Insights** |

---

## Dashboard Architecture & Structure

The dashboard suite is organized into three distinct executive views:

1. **Executive Overview (Macro View):** Top-line ARR, Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Net Revenue Retention (NRR), and Gross Margin.
2. **Sales Funnel & Acquisition:** Channel performance, conversion rates by lead source, sales rep performance, and pipeline velocity.
3. **Product & Engagement:** Daily/Monthly Active Users (DAU/MAU), feature adoption rates, cohort retention curves, and license utilization.

---

## Key Design Principles Applied

* **3-Second Rule:** Primary KPIs are prominently displayed at top-left with immediate color-coded indicator badges (green/red performance vs. targets).
* **Interactive Drill-Downs:** Stakeholders can filter by region, customer segment, or subscription tier with single-click parameters.
* **Self-Serve Export:** Automated scheduled email digests sent directly to leadership every Monday morning at 8:00 AM.

---

## Business Outcomes

* **Faster Strategic Decisions:** Executive meetings shifted from debating data accuracy to discussing strategic growth initiatives.
* **Resource Optimization:** Saved an estimated **600+ hours annually** of analyst manual reporting effort, redirecting team capacity toward proactive strategic deep-dives.
