# Case Study 3: Pricing Tier A/B Test & Revenue Lift

## Executive Summary

To evaluate whether restructuring subscription tiers would increase overall revenue without harming user acquisition, we designed and executed a 4-week randomized controlled A/B test across **50,000 unique prospective customers**.

---

## Hypothesis & Experiment Setup

* **Hypothesis:** Introducing a mid-tier "Professional" subscription tier between Basic ($19/mo) and Enterprise ($99/mo) will increase Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) by encouraging users to upgrade beyond the entry tier.
* **Sample Size:** 50,000 unique visitors split 50/50 between Control and Treatment groups.
* **Duration:** 28 Days.

---

## Variant Configurations

| Feature | Control (Group A) | Treatment (Group B) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier Structure** | 2 Tiers (Basic $19 / Enterprise $99) | 3 Tiers (Basic $19 / Pro $49 / Enterprise $99) |
| **Default Highlight** | Basic ($19) | Pro ($49) marked as "Most Popular" |

---

## Statistical Results & Performance

| Metric | Control (Group A) | Treatment (Group B) | Statistical Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Conversion Rate** | 4.20% | 4.15% | p = 0.68 (Not Significant) |
| **Average Order Value (AOV)** | $24.50 | $30.20 | p < 0.001 (Highly Significant) |
| **Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)**| $1.03 | $1.25 | **+21.3% Lift** (p < 0.01) |
| **Confidence Level** | Baseline | **99% Confidence** | Target Exceeded |

---

## Key Takeaways & Recommendations

* **Conversion Rate Protection:** Overall checkout conversion remained steady (no negative friction introduced by adding a 3rd option).
* **Significant ARPU Expansion:** The $49 Pro tier successfully captured willingness-to-pay from customers who previously settled for the $19 Basic tier.
* **Rollout Decision:** Recommended full 100% rollout of the 3-tier pricing structure across all web and mobile platforms.

---

## Business Impact

* **Projected Revenue Increase:** Projected to generate **+$180,000 in incremental annual recurring revenue (ARR)** upon full launch.
* **Data-Backed Confidence:** Provided leadership with empirical statistical evidence before making major pricing changes, mitigating business risk.
